About Garrett Gabriel Gottfriedson aka GGG

GGG was born and bread in Tk’emlups te Secwepemc Territory. Raised by beautiful mother Abigail Marchand and unforgettable father Late Shawno Gottfriedson. GGG grew up surrounded by art, and no matter where he was, GGG always had his art book!
